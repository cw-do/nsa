We are a group of scientists who specialize in neutron scattering techniques, the study of soft materials, and rigorous data analysis. Our team is dedicated to exploring innovative data analysis methods by integrating artificial intelligence, mathematics, and statistical mechanics to revolutionize the data analysis paradigm for studying soft materials using scattering techniques.

On this site we publish our discussions and share information about upcoming meetings and workshops. Please join us in our journey to advance the field of soft materials research.
