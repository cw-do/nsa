The Neutrons, Soft Matters and Algorithms (NSA) working group is pleased to launch *The Long View*, an ongoing seminar series featuring distinguished scientists whose pioneering contributions have shaped our understanding of soft matter and scattering science.

Many of the theoretical models and analytical methods we routinely use today originated from fundamental questions, physical intuition, and rigorous scientific reasoning. Through this series, we hope to explore how these ideas were originally conceived and developed — how researchers identified important problems, challenged existing assumptions, and transformed physical insights into new theoretical frameworks.

As AI and modern computational tools open new possibilities for scientific discovery, we believe that learning these ways of thinking can inspire a new generation of physics-based models and algorithms.

*The Long View* aims to bridge generations of scientists, preserving the intellectual foundations of our field while inspiring new approaches to the challenges ahead.
