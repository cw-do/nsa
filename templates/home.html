Where this is going

As far as we know, no other group meets where neutron scattering, soft matter and the mathematics of data analysis cross. We began in April 2025 as an online meeting among colleagues. We would like it to grow into a society: a seminar series, workshops, and one day a meeting of its own.

If your work sits here, join us. Write to the organizers to get the meeting announcements, or to propose a talk.
